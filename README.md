# TapColor

Free printable & online coloring pages — for kids, parents, teachers, and anyone who colors to relax.

**Live page:** https://tapcolorapp.github.io/ · **Main app:** https://tapcolor.app

## About

TapColor offers thousands of coloring pages you can **print or color right in your browser** — no sign-up, no watermark, 100% free.

- Thousands of pages: animals, dinosaurs, holidays, mandalas, and more
- Print or color online — no printer required
- Free for home and classroom use

This repository hosts the simple intro page, served via GitHub Pages (`index.html`).

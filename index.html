TapColor

Free coloring pages, for everyone.

We make a large, free library of coloring pages you can print or color online — no sign-up, no watermark, no catch.

Who we are

TapColor is a small project with one simple goal: give kids, parents, teachers, and grown-ups who color to unwind an easy place to find a page and start coloring in seconds.

What we offer

  • Thousands of pages — animals, dinosaurs, holidays, mandalas, and much more.
  • Print or color online — fill pages right in your browser on a phone, tablet, or laptop. No printer required.
  • 100% free — everything is free to use, with no account and no watermark.
  • Made for the classroom too — teachers can print for the whole class in a couple of clicks.

Why we built it

Most coloring sites bury the good pages behind pop-ups, paywalls, and watermarks. We wanted the opposite: open the page, pick a page, color. That's the whole idea — and it stays free.
